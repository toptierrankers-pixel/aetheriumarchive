Hall Three

The Watch for TES VI

Catching up before the next one? See every mainline game in order or how they all rank. No Elder Scrolls game has ever been short on rumor. This page tries to keep the confirmed facts and the community's wishful thinking in two clearly separate piles, and it's worth checking Bethesda's own channels for anything more recent than this Archive's last update.

Confirmed, directly from Bethesda

It exists. That's about it.

Bethesda Game Studios showed a short teaser trailer at E3 2018 confirming The Elder Scrolls VI was in early development. Director Todd Howard has since reiterated, in various interviews, that the studio's priority order ran Starfield first and TES VI after, meaning full production picked up seriously once Starfield shipped. No release date, release window, platforms beyond "eventually current-gen," or gameplay has been officially shown since that 2018 teaser.

Setting The 2018 teaser trailer's landscape shots are widely believed by fans to depict Hammerfell and High Rock, but Bethesda has not confirmed a setting.
Release window Unannounced. Howard has publicly declined to give a date, and past Bethesda estimates for other titles have shifted before release, so treat any date you see elsewhere as a guess, not a fact.
Engine Expected to use the same Creation Engine 2 introduced for Starfield, per statements from the studio.

"It's not like we're not working on it. We are.", Todd Howard, on TES VI's development status, paraphrased from multiple public interviews.

Unconfirmed

What the community is speculating

None of this is official. It's the shape of the conversation among fans right now, treat it as hope, not information.

Setting rumor

Hammerfell & High Rock

The leading fan theory, based on the teaser's terrain and a long-running "next game after Skyrim" wishlist that predates the announcement itself.

Systems rumor

Deeper faction reactivity

Fans are hoping for guild storylines that actually respond to earlier player choices, building on systems teased across Starfield and Fallout 4.

Community wishlist

Everyone has their one big ask

Deeper NPC schedules, meaningful climate and biome variety, playable werewolf-and-vampire progression, real naval travel. The wishlist threads are long, and everyone's list is a little different.

A note on sourcing

Keep your expectations sourced

Elder Scrolls news moves in waves of leaks, official silence, and re-shared old interviews. Before treating anything as fact, including anything on this page, check it against Bethesda's own announcements at bethesda.net or their official social channels. This Archive is a fan project, not a news outlet, and it will occasionally be out of date.
