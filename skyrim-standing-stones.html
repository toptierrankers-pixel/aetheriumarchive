Skyrim Standing Stones Guide: Which One Should You Choose?

Thirteen stones, one power active at a time, and a decision most players make without knowing the full list exists.

What standing stones actually do

Standing stones replaced the birthsign system from earlier Elder Scrolls games. Interacting with one grants a passive bonus or ability, and only one stone's effect can be active at a time, though you're free to switch by visiting a different stone whenever you like, there's no permanent commitment.

The combat stones

  • The Warrior, boosts one-handed, two-handed, archery, and block skill gain.
  • The Lady, boosts health and stamina regeneration, a solid all-around defensive pick.
  • The Steed, removes armor weight penalties and improves carry-focused traits.

The magic stones

  • The Mage, boosts magicka regeneration.
  • The Apprentice, boosts magicka regeneration further but increases magic damage taken, a high-risk pick.
  • The Atronach, grants spell absorption and a larger magicka pool but removes natural magicka regeneration entirely.
  • The Ritual, grants a once-per-day power to raise nearby dead as allies.

The stealth, social, and utility stones

  • The Thief, boosts sneak, lockpicking, pickpocket, and speech gain.
  • The Lover, boosts skill gain rate across the board temporarily after activation.
  • The Lord, grants extra armor and magic resistance.
  • The Shadow, grants a once-per-day invisibility power.
  • The Tower, improves lockpicking and grants bonus gold when opening chests.
  • The Serpent, paralyzes and poisons enemies once per day, a strong niche pick for tough single-target fights.

Picking the right one for your build

Match the stone to your race and playstyle rather than picking blind, a stealth archer built around a Bosmer or Khajiit gets far more value from The Thief than The Mage. If you haven't settled on a build yet, our best race for every build guide is worth reading first.
