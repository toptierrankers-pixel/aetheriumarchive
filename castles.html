The Elder Scrolls: Castles (2024)

Bethesda's newest spinoff swaps dungeon crawling for city building.

A city builder wearing Elder Scrolls clothing

The Elder Scrolls: Castles is the newest entry in the series and easily the biggest departure from the formula. Rather than first-person exploration or dungeon crawling, Castles is a mobile city-builder and dynasty management game, tasking the player with constructing and defending a castle while managing the ruling family across generations.

What the gameplay actually involves

Players build out rooms and structures, assign residents to jobs, manage resources, and make dynastic decisions (marriages, succession, alliances) that shape how the castle and its ruling line evolve over time. It borrows structurally from other mobile management games more than from anything else in the Elder Scrolls catalog, dressed in Tamriel's setting and lore references.

Why it's a good SEO and community opportunity right now

Because Castles is so new, there's very little written content covering it in depth compared to the rest of the series. That's an opportunity worth revisiting and expanding on as the game gets patched and as the community's reception becomes clearer over time.

Should you play it today?

If you enjoy management and city-builder games and want that experience with an Elder Scrolls skin, it's worth trying, it's free to download. If you're looking for a traditional Elder Scrolls RPG experience, this isn't it, and you're better served by any of the other seven entries on this list.
